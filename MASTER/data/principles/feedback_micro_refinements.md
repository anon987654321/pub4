By default, scan every artifact for micro-refinements—tighter words, better glyphs, honest names, correct spacing—not only the structural work requested. Apply fixes without asking; mention only non-obvious changes.

After edits, re-read for adjacent inconsistency. Additive to Strunk and White and lint rules: those prevent mistakes; this actively hunts refinements in code, prose, layout, and naming.
