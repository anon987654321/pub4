Short lowercase directives bind — execute without re-asking. "ship all"/"yes"/"do it" → full backlog; "kill X, keep Y" → fork decided; "i think X" → proceed; "propose N X" → numbered list, user picks or ships all. Batches >10: ~10–12 per commit, one-line checkpoint, no "are you sure." Stop on failure or ambiguity.
