Short lowercase directives are binding authorization—execute without re-asking. Ship all, yes, and do it mean run the full backlog; kill X keep Y means the fork is decided; i think X means proceed; propose N X means deliver a numbered list for the user to pick from or ship all.

For batches larger than ten items, commit roughly ten to twelve per pass with a one-line checkpoint and no are-you-sure prompts. Stop only on failure or genuine ambiguity.
