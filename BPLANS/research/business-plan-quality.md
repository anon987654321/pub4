# Research: what makes a good business plan good

For the pub4.good restoration — November 2026. Sources actually consulted:
innovasjonnorge.no (oppstartstilskudd-1 service page, forretningsmodell article),
info.altinn.no (forretningsmodell, budsjett), starte-as.no, fiken.no blogg,
tripletex.no, UE Budsjettguiden, SBA.gov (market research / competitive
analysis), LivePlan ("10 Qualities That Make a Good Business Plan"), and the
academic review Nakajima (2025), "Is Business Planning Useful for
Entrepreneurs? A Review", *Administrative Sciences* 5(1) (mdpi.com).

## 1. The five axes Innovasjon Norge actually scores

Innovasjon Norge evaluates applications on five axes (as summarised by
advisors working with their process): **innovasjon**, **marked**,
**gjennomføringsevne**, **vekstpotensial** and **behovet for støtte**. Their
own service pages for Oppstartstilskudd 1 (up to NOK 150 000) spell out six
documentation requirements that read as the operational version of the same
five axes:

| Criterion | What they want to see | Documented by |
|---|---|---|
| Problemforståelse / kundeinnsikt | A real, important problem; what it costs customers in time, money or work; **3–5 concrete insights from customer conversations** | Pilot data, interviews |
| Løsning og usikkerhet | What you will build, what makes it difficult, a real development path with uncertainty and news value | Sketch, product description |
| Dagens alternativ | How the problem is solved today and why that falls short | Competitor analysis/matrix |
| Segment og marked | A clear first customer group, how you reach them, growth incl. international | Market tables, go-to-market |
| IPR | What is unique, what can be protected, why it is hard to copy | Patent/trademark status |
| Team og gjennomføring | Who runs it, what competence exists, **what must be bought in** | Team bios + external needs |

The pub4 plans already scored on løsning, dagens alternativ (Konkurranse),
segment (Kundesegmenter) and IPR (Immaterielle rettigheter). The completion
closes the documented gaps: kundeinnsikt (each plan now cites its own pilot
evidence — SYRE's 50 pilot users, pub.healthcare's Tromsø prototype and
Haukeland pilot, govt_bergen's 500 test users and 15 pilot votes, Norwegian
Hedge's 6-month Sharpe-2.1 paper trading, Ragnhild's three sold coffins with
five-star reviews, SPEIS/NATO's SINTEF simulations), go-to-market (Markedsføring
og salg), gjennomføring (Milepæler og måltall + Behov for ekstern kompetanse)
and the financial documentation (three budgets, see §3).

## 2. What practitioners and the research literature say

- **Fit, realism, specificity.** LivePlan's ten qualities: the plan fits the
  business need, is realistic and implementable, is specific and measurable,
  defines responsibilities, documents assumptions, and stays current as a
  living document rather than a one-off document.
- **Planning works when it is concrete.** The 2025 literature review (Nakajima)
  finds the evidence is mixed but converges on this: formal planning correlates
  with growth, but only when it contains specific, measurable commitments and
  is revised as the business learns — which is also exactly how Altinn frames
  the forretningsmodell ("et levende dokument"). Vague ambition documents show
  no effect. This is why the pub4 completions bind every claim to an existing
  figure in the same plan and add measurable targets (milepæler, KPI-er).
- **Market research before prose.** SBA's framework: demand, market size,
  economic indicators, location, saturation, pricing — then competitive
  analysis by segment: market share, strengths/weaknesses, window of
  opportunity, barriers, indirect competitors. The pub4 plans already carry
  the market-size table and competitor list; the completion adds the "window
  of opportunity" argument (Hvorfor nå) and the pricing-based unit economics.
- **Business Model Canvas as the IN-preferred skeleton.** IN recommends
  canvas thinking: value proposition, customer segments, channels, customer
  relationships, revenue streams, resources, activities, partners, costs. The
  eight pub4 sections already walk this in prose; the additions make the
  channels (go-to-market) and unit economics explicit.

## 3. The Norwegian three-budget convention

Norwegian practice (Altinn, starte-as.no, Fiken, Tripletex, Universitetet i
Sørøst-Norge's Budsjettguiden) is unambiguous: a forretningsplan carries
**three** budgets:

1. **Resultatbudsjett** — expected income statement over the period;
2. **Investeringsbudsjett** — what the capital is spent on;
3. **Likviditetsbudsjett** — the cash flow, so the company can see it always
   has money on the account ("sikre at selskapet alltid har penger på konto").

The pub4 plans had only the funding allocation, which satisfies (2) but not
(1) and (3). The completion states the allocation as the investeringsbudsjett
and adds three-year resultatbudsjett and likviditetsbudsjett tables in NOK
millioner with carried-forward balances. All arithmetic is machine-verified:
driftsresultat = inntekter − kostnader, resultat før skatt = driftsresultat +
finansposter, utgående saldo = inngående + innbetalinger − utbetalinger −
investeringer + kapitalinnbetaling, and each year's utgående saldo equals the
next year's inngående saldo.

Revenue paths are anchored in each plan's own roadmap and text (SYRE:
break-even at 800 pairs ≈ 18 months, 25 % net margin at full drift; Ragnhild:
NOK 6M revenue / NOK 2.1M profit at 200 ceremonies in year 3; Norwegian Hedge:
NOK 16.5M income at NOK 500M AUM year 3; govt_bergen: NOK 15M revenue 2027,
break-even at 5 municipalities + Bergen in year 2; SPEIS/NATO/pub.healthcare:
milestone-based revenue recognition on long-term contracts with customer
prepayments noted in the liquidity budget).

## 4. What the risk section must do

The research and the IN criteria agree on what a credible risk section looks
like: name the risk, rate probability and consequence, and — the part most
plans forget — tie each mitigation to something already in the plan (budget
lines, partners, architecture). The completion adds a four-row risk matrix per
plan where the tiltak column only references things the plan already pays for
(e.g. govt_bergen's ConsenSys audit = the 20 % security line; pub.healthcare's
certification budget = the 10 % sertifisering line; Norwegian Hedge's legal
budget = the 16,7 % regulatorisk line).
