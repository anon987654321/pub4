# MASTER

## Constitutional Engineering System

MASTER is not an autocomplete framework.
MASTER is an adversarial engineering environment designed for:

- durability
- auditability
- reversibility
- operational clarity
- bounded complexity
- long-term maintainability
- hostile review survivability

The system assumes:

- humans are tired
- future maintainers lack context
- abstractions leak
- dependencies decay
- LLMs hallucinate
- cleverness becomes operational debt
- autonomous systems partially fail continuously
