# EriTel commercial model

This document describes mechanisms to discuss with EriTel. It is not a proposed final price list.

## Registry economics

Separate:
- registry wholesale fee;
- registrar or reseller margin;
- payment processing;
- support cost;
- DNS service cost;
- abuse operations;
- taxes and regulatory costs;
- refunds and chargebacks.

Every transaction should be reconcilable from customer order to registry result to settlement.

## Possible arrangements

### Registrar

The partner pays EriTel an agreed wholesale amount and operates the retail relationship.

### Reseller

The partner sells under an EriTel-approved reseller structure while EriTel controls the underlying registrar relationship.

### Technical services

Fees are tied to explicitly contracted infrastructure or operational services.

### Revenue sharing

Revenue share should define:
- gross versus net basis;
- currency;
- settlement cycle;
- refunds;
- chargebacks;
- taxes;
- reporting;
- audit rights.

## Financial controls

The system should retain a transaction ledger that can reconcile:
order -> payment -> registry operation -> renewal -> refund -> settlement

No financial number should be presented to EriTel as actual until it is derived from recorded transactions.
