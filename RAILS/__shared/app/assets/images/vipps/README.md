# Vipps brand assets

**Two official Vipps wordmarks, unmodified, as Vipps MobilePay publishes them
in its own merchant plugin; every app on the shared engine uses these.**

- `vipps-wordmark.svg`: orange wordmark for light backgrounds.
  Source: https://github.com/vippsas/vipps-woocommerce/blob/052b7fd57365ec9c68e91a7b60df7af210bb61d2/payment/img/vipps-rgb-orange-pos.svg
- `vipps-wordmark-on-orange.svg`: white wordmark on its own Vipps-orange
  block, for buttons and badges.
  Source: https://github.com/vippsas/vipps-woocommerce/blob/052b7fd57365ec9c68e91a7b60df7af210bb61d2/payment/img/vipps-rgb-orange-neg.svg

They sit beside Vipps login and payment so people recognise the method.
Use them as they are (no recolouring or stretching) and replace them with the
files from the merchant agreement's brand kit when that arrives. mrdamascy is
not on the shared engine and keeps its own copy of `vipps-wordmark.svg`.
