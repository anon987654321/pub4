Here is how to train the Ragnhild portrait LoRA on RunPod.

Before you start: create a Hugging Face token and accept the FLUX one point dev license online. Push your latest pub4 code to GitHub. The seventeen-image dataset is already in the repo, but you still need to train fresh weights. There are no FLUX safetensors checked in yet.

On RunPod, choose a GPU with at least twenty-four gigabytes of video memory. An RTX forty-ninety, A five thousand, or L four is ideal. Pick a PyTorch CUDA twelve template and allocate eighty gigabytes of disk so the FLUX download fits comfortably.

SSH into the pod and export your Hugging Face token. Then run the setup script from pub4's lora training folder with the train flag. That installs dependencies, clones ai-toolkit, runs the gate check, and starts training inside a tmux session named ragnhild.

Monitor with tmux attach, or tail the train run log. Checkpoints land every two hundred fifty steps in the weights folder. The full run is eighteen hundred steps.

When training completes, copy the safetensors files back to your Mac with secure copy. Then terminate the pod immediately so you are not billed for idle GPU time.

On your Mac, run generate and postpro to produce the final portrait images in the lora folder. Skip postpro on the pod; portrait grading uses MASTER postpro on the Mac.

Expect two to five hours on CUDA. Local Apple Silicon training was too slow and kept failing without Hugging Face access. RunPod is the right path for this project.
