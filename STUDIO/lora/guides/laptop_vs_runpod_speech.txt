Laptop versus RunPod for training the Ragnhild portrait LoRA.

Both paths run the same pipeline: seventeen curated images, rank thirty-two LoRA, eighteen hundred steps, on FLUX one point dev. The only switch is the device. Your Mac uses M P S, Apple Metal. RunPod uses C U D A on an NVIDIA GPU.

On the laptop, render config automatically throttles the job. Quantization and low V RAM mode stay on, because FLUX is enormous and unified memory is tight. The optimizer falls back to full Adam W, because eight-bit Adam W only works on C U D A. Precision drops to F P sixteen, because B F sixteen can produce NaN loss on M P S. Learning rate lowers to one times ten to the minus five. And training resolution collapses to five twelve pixels only, instead of five twelve, seven sixty-eight, and ten twenty-four.

Your Mac actually tried this. It passed the Hugging Face gate, downloaded FLUX, loaded the transformer and T five encoder, then crashed during latent caching with a Metal compiler service error. Training never reached step one. So the laptop is not slow but workable. On your machine, it failed.

RunPod sets the device to C U D A and runs the full recipe. No quantization hacks. Eight-bit Adam W. B F sixteen. Learning rate five times ten to the minus five. Multi-resolution buckets at five twelve, seven sixty-eight, and ten twenty-four. You want at least twenty-four gigabytes of video memory, PyTorch with C U D A twelve, and fifty to eighty gigabytes of disk for the model cache and checkpoints. Setup runs in a tmux session named ragnhild, so you can close your laptop and training keeps going.

The practical split is simple. Use the Mac for checks, and for generate and postpro after you have weights. Use RunPod for the one heavy step that actually finishes. Train on the pod, secure-copy the safetensors files back, terminate the pod so you stop paying, then run generate and postpro on the Mac for the final portraits.

Time and cost: on your Mac, model load took thirty plus minutes and latent caching failed. On a twenty-four gigabyte RunPod GPU, expect roughly one to three hours of training and about one to three dollars total if you kill the pod when done.

Bottom line: you are not choosing two different LoRAs. You are choosing survival mode on the Mac versus intended mode on RunPod. For Ragnhild v two, RunPod is the train step. The Mac is the finish step.
