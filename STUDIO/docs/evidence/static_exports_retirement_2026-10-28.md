# Static exports retirement — RAILS/\_\_other deleted, 2026-10-28

Scope: `RAILS/__other/` (Sommerseth Eiendom on thor.brgen.no, Alfa Bergen on
alfa.brgen.no), both declared obsolete by the operator. Everything the two
sites pointed at was removed with them; the design decisions they carried
were kept where they suit the RAILS apps.

## Deleted

- `RAILS/__other/` — both static trees (HTML/CSS/JS/fonts/images/screenshots)
  and the folder README. The two `assets/motion.js` files were byte-identical;
  their contract is recorded in `MASTER/docs/visual/bergen_brand_systems.md`.
- `RAILS/apps.yml` — the whole `static_exports` section (both rows were
  `done`, activated 2026-10-28, and are now historical).
- `OPENBSD/etc/httpd.conf` — the two loopback vhosts (38191/38192).
- `OPENBSD/etc/relayd.conf` — both tables, the thor/alfa SAN keypairs, both
  Host matches, both forwards, and the first-activation checklist comment
  (replaced by a retirement note).
- `OPENBSD/etc/acme-client.conf` — `thor.brgen.no` and `alfa.brgen.no` from
  the brgen.no SAN list.
- `OPENBSD/var/nsd/zones/master/brgen.no.zone` — the alfa and thor A records;
  serial bumped to 2026102802 (zone is generated by `render_dns.rb`, which
  regenerates this exact output from the shortened `ALL_DOMAINS`).
- `OPENBSD/OPERATOR.sh` — `thor,alfa` removed from the brgen.no
  `ALL_DOMAINS` entry.
- `OPENBSD/bin/vps-deploy-static` + `OPENBSD/man/man8/vps-deploy-static.8` —
  the deploy entrypoint existed only for these two trees (the Rails apps have
  `vps-deploy`); `MASTER/test/test_manual_pages.rb` inventory updated.

## Design decisions kept, and where they landed

The operator asked to keep any design decision suited for the RAILS apps.

Kept into `RAILS/__shared` (reaches every app that mounts the engine):

- **Print guarantee** → `_animations.scss`, beside the reduced-motion reset:
  `@media print` kills animation/transition state and forces the visible trio
  on scroll-reveal targets and `--delay`-staggered elements. Measured failure
  on the source sites: print/PDF never fires IntersectionObserver, so
  below-fold sections printed blank. `_interaction_craft.scss` resolves the
  success-check dash under print for the same reason.
- **`::selection` as an identity surface** → `_base.scss`, one owner beside
  `:focus-visible`, painted with the existing `--accent`/`--accent-ink` pair
  — no new colours, no palette change.

Kept in place (already the apps' law):

- Self-hosted OFL-1.1 fonts with `font-display: swap` (`_fonts.scss` ships
  Source Sans 3 + JetBrainsMono the same way).
- Reduced-motion global reset including `scroll-behavior: auto`
  (`_animations.scss`, 2026-09-03).
- One `:focus-visible` owner (`_focus_ring.scss`), 44 px tap floor
  (`--tap-min`), `tabular-numeric` counters (`_chat_reactions`,
  `_shared_coverage_fills`, `_surface_chrome_endgame`).

Deliberately not ported (site identities, not decisions): the paper/ink/
signal palettes, the Bergen-snittet v1 mark parameters, custom cursor +
ring, word masks, marquee, magnetic buttons, parallax frames. The apps'
monochrome one-scheme is operator-owned; porting a second identity into it
would violate RAILS/CLAUDE.md.

## Law calibration for RAILS sweeps (/fix readiness)

`MASTER/law/brand.rb` keeps all six laws; provenance header updated. Measured
over the four apps, three detectors mis-read the apps' own scheme as drift
and are now scoped by `laws.yml path_pattern` to brand-export trees
(`/__other/`), where they sleep until such a tree exists again:

- `SIGNAL_COLOR_NAMING`: 11 hits on canonical `--accent:` declarations
  (`_dialect_tokens.scss`, per-app application.scss, master face.css).
- `PAPER_NOT_PURE_WHITE`: 17 hits on the deliberate `#ffffff` light ground
  and mailer literals.
- `PARAMETRIC_MARK_ONLY`: 17 hits on shared icon partials and the
  marketplace animated logo — artwork, not brand marks.

The other three walk RAILS/** universally and measured zero findings:
`CURSOR_NONE_REQUIRES_CURSOR`, `NORWEGIAN_COPY_BOKMAL`,
`WORDMASK_OBSERVES_UNCLIPPED`.

Data retired with the sites: `tokens.yml#tokens.brand_systems` removed
(`configuration_guard.rb` REQUIRED_TOKEN_MAPS updated). The doctrine doc
`MASTER/docs/visual/bergen_brand_systems.md` stays as the decision record
with a retirement note and a rewritten /fix runbook.

## Domain ghosts go silent

`OPENBSD/lib/domain_signals.rb` flagged both names as `relay_residual`
(Host matches in relayd.conf outside the known set). `MASTER/test/
test_domain_signals.rb` asserted that residual on purpose; it now asserts
the empty set — every Host match on the relay is a known name.
